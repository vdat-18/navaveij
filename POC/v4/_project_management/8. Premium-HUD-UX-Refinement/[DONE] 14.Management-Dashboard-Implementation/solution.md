# Solution — The Control Room Dashboard & Metrics

**Date:** 2026-03-30

## Detailed Features & Metrics Breakdown

### 1. Overview Metrics HUD
Provides a high-level view of financial performance and AI interaction levels:
- **Gross Revenue:** Total revenue generated from all examinations and consults.
- **Uplift Value:** Additional net revenue purely generated through the AI's add-on recommendations.
- **Rec. Items (Recommended Items):** The total volume of extra services suggested by the AI system.
- **Packages Sold:** The number of AI-recommended service packages that patients actually purchased.
- **Adoption Rate:** The percentage of AI recommendations that nurses/physicians accepted and presented to patients (instead of ignoring or rejecting).
- **Efficiency:** The conversion rate—the percentage of accepted recommendations that resulted in a successful patient agreement/sale.

### 2. Clinical Outcome Yield (Weekly Performance Chart)
- **Function:** A bar chart illustrating the correlation between "AI Recommendations" and "Uplift Revenue" week over week.
- **Purpose:** Helps management evaluate whether the increased volume of consultations is translating into proportionate financial returns, thereby accurately assessing ROI.

### 3. Rejection Diagnostics
- **Function:** Tracks the percentages of reasons why AI suggestions are rejected (e.g., Patient refusal, Cost concerns, Already examined elsewhere, Doctor's override).
- **Purpose:** Pinpoints exactly where the bottleneck is. If cost is the main issue, management can adjust the AI to recommend more affordable packages. If patient refusal is high, it highlights a need for better consultation training.

### 4. Operational Excellence Protocol (Global Practice Adoption Table)
- **Function:** A detailed roster categorizing individual Nurses/Physicians alongside their personal KPIs:
  - **Adoption Intensity:** How consistently the user accepts AI suggestions (with a progress bar).
  - **Efficiency Delta:** The precise fraction of successful consultations vs. total assigned recommendations.
  - **Value Generation:** The exact monetary uplift revenue directly generated by this individual.
- **Purpose:** Allows the Chief Medical Officer or Clinic Manager to instantly identify "Top Adopters" and pinpoint those who need 1-on-1 coaching due to poor AI interaction or weak consultation skills. The color-coded performance tiers (Green/Yellow/Red) encourage a performance-driven culture.
