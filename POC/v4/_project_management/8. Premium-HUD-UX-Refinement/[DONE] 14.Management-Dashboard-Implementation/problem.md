# Problem — Lack of High-Level Oversight

**Date:** 2026-03-30

## Current State
- The system provided patient-level clinical recommendations but lacked an aggregated view for hospital management.
- Managers had no way to track how much revenue the AI was generating or who the top adopters were.

## Pain Points
- Cannot see return on investment (ROI) for the AI system in real-time.
- Difficult to identify why recommendations are being rejected.
- Hard to locate practitioners who need additional AI coaching.
